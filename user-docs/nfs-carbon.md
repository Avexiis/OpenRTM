# Need for Speed: Carbon

[Other Games](other-games.md) | [All guides](README.md)

Select Carbon while the Xbox 360 game is running. Load your career; enter free roam before using pursuit or current-car actions.

| Section | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Career | Refresh, add, remove, or set Cash; Skip New Career Intro. |
| Pursuit | Heat from 1.0 to 5.0, Cops enabled/disabled, Start Pursuit, and Prevent Bust. |
| Driving | Infinite Speedbreaker, Infinite Nitrous, and Freeze Opponents. |

Use the [shared controls](other-games.md#shared-controls) for applying cash and heat. Skip New Career Intro affects a new career.

Carbon does not offer a bounty editor, yellow-filter setting, or fast-travel controls on this page. Keep a career backup and disable temporary driving changes when finished.
